Business Perspective

Understanding the business,
not only the dataset.

Good analysis should be connected to a decision. I focus on the business problem, the stakeholder, the metric that matters, and what a practical next step could look like.

I approach analytics as
decision support.

Before starting analysis, I try to understand the purpose behind the request: Is the team trying to improve sales performance, understand customers, reduce reporting gaps, or prioritize limited resources?

This business-first perspective helps me choose relevant metrics, avoid analysis without context, and communicate findings as clear recommendations rather than technical output alone.
