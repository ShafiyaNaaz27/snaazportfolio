03 — Business perspective

Understand the decision.
Then explore the data.

A useful analysis needs context. I aim to understand what the team wants to improve before choosing metrics, visuals, or analytical methods.

I approach analytics
as decision support.

Is the goal to understand customer behavior, compare sales performance, investigate a reporting gap, or prioritize resources? Each question calls for a different analytical approach.

I try to connect findings to a clear next step, explain limitations, and avoid presenting assumptions as proven business outcomes.

Objective What decision needs support?
Metric How will improvement be evaluated?
Evidence What does the data actually show?
Action What practical next step follows?
